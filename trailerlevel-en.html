How it works

Three steps, about a minute.

Once it is mounted, this is the whole routine every time you arrive at a site.

1
Plug it in
Any 5 V USB source — the socket in your trailer, or a power bank. The unit boots in a few seconds. A cable is included.
2
Join its Wi-Fi
Pick the unit's network on your phone. The level screen opens by itself. No app, no login, no pairing.

Details

Built around the things that actually go wrong.

🎯
0.1° resolution
Pitch and roll, updated live over a websocket. Fine enough to chase the last half degree out of an absorption fridge.
⚖
Two-point calibration
Mount it crooked and it does not matter. A one-minute routine cancels out how the unit happens to sit — and you do not need a level surface to do it.
🌡
Temperature compensation
MEMS sensors drift with temperature. The unit records the temperature at calibration and corrects for it — and warns you on screen if it has moved too far.
🔄
Updates over the air
New firmware is pushed from the same web page. No USB cable, no computer, no driver hunting.

FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

Does my phone lose internet while I use it?
Yes — while you are joined to the unit's Wi-Fi, that phone is not on the internet. Levelling takes a minute or two, and most phones fall back to mobile data for other apps. If you want to stay online, use a second device to read the level.
Trailer or motorhome?
Both. It measures the attitude of whatever it is mounted to. Caravans, travel trailers, fifth wheels, motorhomes, van conversions — all the same job.
Does it need to be mounted perfectly straight?
No. That is what the two-point calibration is for. Stick it down wherever is convenient and calibrate once.
Can I leave it plugged in all the time?
It is designed to be switched on when you arrive and off when you leave. It is not intended for permanent power or for use while driving.
What if it breaks?
Message me. I built it, so I can fix it or replace it. Firmware problems can usually be solved with an over-the-air update.
Why is it so much cheaper than the big brands?
No retail margin, no packaging design, no marketing budget, no app to maintain. It is one person, a 3D printer, and a soldering iron.
